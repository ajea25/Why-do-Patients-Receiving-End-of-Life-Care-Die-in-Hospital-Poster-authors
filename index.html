    Valérie GrattonMD

    Palliative care consultant · Assistant Professor

    1 Hôpital Montfort2 Institut du Savoir Montfort3 Division of Palliative Care, Department of Medicine, University of Ottawa

    Dr. Valérie Gratton is a palliative care consultant at Hôpital Montfort, where she founded the palliative care service in 2011. She completed her family medicine residency and an additional year of palliative care training at the University of Ottawa. As Assistant Professor in the Division of Palliative Care, she coordinates undergraduate palliative care education and teaches medical students, residents and fellows. Her research focuses on palliative care for non-cancer illnesses, new models that bring palliative care into every stage of the care continuum, communication in palliative care, and the end-of-life needs of French-speaking Ontarians.
